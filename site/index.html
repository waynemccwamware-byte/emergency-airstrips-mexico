Planning Emergency Airstrips in Mexico

v0.19

Purpose: Enter a route across Mexico and see the airstrips along it, filtered by runway length, surface and permit, so you know your options before you go.

Web app: plan on the ground. It needs an internet connection and keeps nothing for offline use. In flight, you have only what you've installed in ForeFlight.

Route

Waypoints separated by spaces: ICAO airports (MMLT, KSDM), VOR/NDB idents (SQN), AFAC airstrips with an X in front (XSQA; a bare 3-letter ident is taken as a navaid first, as in ForeFlight), coordinates (2830N11030W or 28.5/-110.5), or fix/radial/distance (PPE/110/30: 30 NM out on PPE's 110 radial, magnetic, with today's variation at the fix, as ForeFlight places it). Where an ident fits more than one place, the one nearest the previous waypoint is used.

Distance along the route and off course (left or right of the course line) in NM. "Open to third parties" is AFAC's service type "particular y a terceros" (private-use permits are "particular" only); ICAO airports pass it, and "Current permit only", when they're on Mexico's official airport list. ICAO runway length and surface come from OurAirports. Airstrips, elevations and lengths as AFAC's list gives them (current edition); AFAC's figures are sometimes wrong, and an airstrip on this list is not a known-safe landing spot. To draw one for ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot or X-Plane, look it up in Mexican Airstrips for your EFB and Sim.

Before you fly — this app won't work without a signal. Only what's in the installed pack goes with you.
  • Finish the install: ForeFlight only queues the pack. Go to More → Custom Content and tap Install.
  • Test it offline: turn on airplane mode, open Maps, and check the layers and a _EMG waypoint's record.
  • Only what you ticked is in the pack. Airstrips you unticked or filtered out stay in this app. The red "longest stretch with nothing in the corridor" goes in the pack's Route layer.
  • It's a snapshot as of . Permits and AFAC's list change. Rebuild the pack before each trip.
  • Download ForeFlight's own maps (and satellite imagery, if you use it) for the area. Otherwise these layers draw over a blank map.

On iPhone or iPad, "Open in ForeFlight" sends the pack to ForeFlight (it asks first). ForeFlight then only queues the pack: to finish, go to More → Custom Content, tap the pack, and tap Install. If tapping the button doesn't open ForeFlight, press and hold it and choose "Open in ForeFlight". On a Mac, PC or Android device, ForeFlight isn't there, so download the .zip and AirDrop or email it to the iPad, then open it in ForeFlight. In the Maps layer selector each file in the pack is its own toggle (Route, Corridor, Airstrips); the _EMG waypoints file without their layer on, so you can type them into a flight plan. Content packs need an individual ForeFlight subscription, or Performance level or higher for Business and MFB accounts. Sending this route again? ForeFlight doesn't replace a pack of the same name; it installs a second copy beside the old one. So first take its waypoints out of any active flight plan, delete the old pack in ForeFlight, close and reopen ForeFlight, then send it again.

    Needs an internet connection: a planning tool for use on the ground. In flight, rely only on what you've installed in ForeFlight. Not for navigation. A planning aid only. Data: AFAC's "Aeródromos y helipuertos" list (gob.mx), Mexico's official airport list with positions from OurAirports, and OurAirports navaids and US airports (public domain). With thanks to airports.robrc.net, which showed the way to AFAC's data. Check every airstrip against current official sources, recent pilot reports and your own eyes.
